## The entry

> One star for gravity. Works perfectly until my phone lands face-down, and customer service never answers.

A deadpan one-star review of gravity, the everyday essential nobody can
opt out of. The grievance is the sharpest true one everyone recognises:
gravity never fails — except that everything you drop lands on its worst
side. The sting arrives in the last few words, filed like a real
complaint against a support desk that does not exist.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the single-line review (105 characters, within the
160-character limit) to `/output/entry.txt`.
